01WHAT RADI0 COLLECTS — AND WHY

RADI0 is a live radio service. It collects the minimum required to run a station where votes are honest and one person counts once.

ANONYMOUS LISTENER ID
A random identifier (UUID) generated on your device. It is used for listener counts and vote integrity. It is not derived from — and not linked to — your name, email, or any account.
SIGN IN WITH APPLE
If you sign in, Apple provides RADI0 a stable user identifier. Your name is optional. Your email is received only if you choose to share it. This identity is used solely as the one-person-one-vote key — and, for station staff, to verify role. It is not used for anything else.
VOTES
Boosts and buries are stored tied to your identity so that each person counts once. Votes are what programs the station.
MICROPHONE
Accessed only while you use call-in or host broadcasting. In those moments your audio is streamed and uploaded — that is the feature. The microphone is not touched at any other time.
CAMERA
Accessed only while a host runs a camera broadcast. Never otherwise.
LISTENING
Requires no account. You can listen without telling us who you are.
02WHAT WE DON'T DO
  • NO ADSThere is no advertising in RADI0.
  • NO DATA SALEWe do not sell your data. Not to anyone, not in aggregate.
  • NO CROSS-APP TRACKINGWe do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • NO LOCATIONWe do not collect your location.
03RETENTION

Votes and play history are retained as station records — they are the station's programming log. To request deletion of data tied to your identity, contact the address below and we will remove it.
