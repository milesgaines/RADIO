RADI0 · SUPPORT
QHOW DOES LISTENING WORK?
Free. No account. Open the app — or the web player — pick a station, press play. One stream per station; everyone hears the same second.
QHOW DOES VOTING WORK?
Sign in with Apple inside the app. One person, one vote. BOOST keeps a record in rotation; BURY pushes it out. The station plays what the room holds up.
QWHY CAN'T I SKIP?
Because it's radio. There is one playhead per station and every listener shares it. If a record should go, bury it — when enough of the room agrees, it goes.
QHOW DO I GO LIVE?
Broadcasting is limited to station operators and staff. When a host opens the phone line, you can request to call in from inside the app.
QHOW DO REPLAYS WORK?
Finished broadcasts are filed under SHOWS → THE ARCHIVE in the app. The board on the web lists the same archive. Replays stream the same way live shows do.
